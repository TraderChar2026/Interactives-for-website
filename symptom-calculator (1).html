Cellular Health Clinical Intake

A structured intake to help identify which cellular signaling pathways may be worth discussing with a patient — combining reported symptoms, clinical history and risk factors, and objective lab markers where available, rather than symptoms alone.

Educational use only — not a diagnostic tool. This intake does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and is not a substitute for clinical judgment, laboratory testing, or a full patient history. Objective markers are optional — leave them unchecked if not yet tested. Statements have not been evaluated by the FDA.
